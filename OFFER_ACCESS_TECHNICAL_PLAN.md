## Date and payment rules

- Annual cutoff is an inclusive date in Africa/Tunis. Convert the following local midnight to a timestamp. For 9 June 2027, access expires at the start of 10 June in Tunis. Reject checkout or confirmation when the chosen cutoff has passed.
- Monthly access starts at confirmation. Its expiry is the beginning of the next month’s corresponding local calendar date in Africa/Tunis. If that date does not exist, clamp to the destination month’s last date. Save the initial day as an anchor across contiguous renewals, so 31 January 2027 → 28 February → 31 March, and in a leap year the February cutoff is the 29th. For 30 January the sequence is 28 February → 30 March; for 29 January it is 28 February → 29 March. Access lasts through the preceding local date because these are exclusive midnight expiries. A new purchase after a lapse takes a fresh anchor from its actual confirmation date. Early renewals extend from the existing expiry; allow at most one queued period. Requote if the Tunis date, terms or renewal state changes before confirmation.
- Student checkout derives the account from its signed session. Lock the student, then the offer, and recheck terms and current grants. Write the payment, grant, course snapshot, progress rows and ledger in one transaction. A request ID and the student lock prevent duplicate charges or grants. No automatic debits.
- Installment drafts are available only for timed offers with an approved minimum initial payment. Confirmation is a separate admin action. Under the same lock order, check the arrangement, initial amount and actual payment source, then record the payment and debt and create the grant atomically. Start access only at that confirmation. Record actor and time. A repeated confirmation returns the existing grant. Disabled, expired or changed terms are rechecked before any grant; an already granted period survives subsequent offer edits.
- Access checks accept an existing valid independent enrollment, or any timed grant whose window includes the current time and covers the requested course. Offer-scoped live access additionally requires the corresponding offer grant. Review all lesson, signed resource, live/replay, progress, enrollment and catalog queries listed in the source audit. Never treat a timed-only progress row as a legacy entitlement.
- A periodic worker uses the existing pool and inserts a French reminder once per grant when the approved expiry is within three days and still in the future. Use a single insert with conflict handling, so restarts and multiple workers cannot duplicate it. Skip revoked grants and grants already covered by a queued renewal. The message links to the offers page and says that progress is saved.

## Installment servicing and money

1. Create a draft in the existing student details dialog. Show quoted price and minimum initial payment. The draft creates no charge, debt, due date or access.
2. An authorized admin selects wallet or verifies an external payment, enters the confirmed amount, and explicitly grants access. An optional next payment date can be set at confirmation. Once the grant is issued, price, payment records and access expiry are fixed.
3. For an outstanding arrangement, an admin can choose Modifier l’échéance, enter the next due date and provide a reason. The system records the old date, new date, actor and time. This only changes the payment deadline. A balance is overdue once that date ends in Tunis and money remains due. No automatic suspension, revocation, late fee or debit.
4. An admin can choose Enregistrer un paiement, select the source, review the exact amount and confirm. Accept only positive amounts up to the remaining balance. A partial payment reduces the outstanding amount; a full payment clears the next due date and settles the arrangement. Additional payments do not extend or restart access. Paid records remain immutable.
5. When annual access ends, any unpaid balance remains collectible. A later payment settles the debt without reopening access. The system applies no automatic write-off or penalty.
6. Keep timed installment debt on its arrangement. Do not write it into users.debt, which remains the legacy aggregate used by the existing legacy collection action. The admin details can show both balances separately; the legacy action cannot accidentally settle a timed arrangement.
7. For the new timed flows, exchange monetary values as canonical decimal strings with at most two fractional digits, consistent with existing NUMERIC(10,2) storage. Reject extra precision. Perform validation, differences and updates in PostgreSQL NUMERIC; do not use parseFloat, JavaScript Number arithmetic or binary rounding. Existing legacy monetary handlers are outside this change.
8. Lock student, offer and arrangement in that order where relevant. Verify identity, terms, minimum, balance and prior request before writing. Wallet debit, transaction, payment, paid total/debt and grant commit together on confirmation. A duplicate identical request returns the original result. Reusing an ID with different input returns a conflict. Additional collection and deadline changes have separate idempotency keys and run under the arrangement lock. Database uniqueness and the lock also prevent two different confirmation requests from granting twice.

## Offer upgrades with installment credit — revised proposal

Status: business rules approved as revised. Final technical approval pending. This section authorizes documentation only.

### Eligibility and offer configuration

- In Modifier l’offre, configure installments independently from upgrades. For newly configured offers, installments disabled permits one payment. Enabled permits up to the configured maximum of two or three, including the student's choice of one. Annual academic-year offers are the only upgrade destinations in the first release.
- Configure each source-to-target pair separately. Full payment is required by default. A pair can explicitly permit verified partial credit. Show outstanding source debt separately. Preserve the original payment obligations, grants and expiry dates.
- Monthly offers are permanently excluded as credit sources, including purchases, renewals and installment collections. The source picker hides monthly offers and has no override. Monthly students may buy an annual offer at full price using its permitted schedule. Existing monthly access remains valid through its recorded expiry.
- API validation rejects a monthly source even if the request bypasses the UI. Database triggers must also reject monthly source rules and claims against monthly payment origins. Check the source offer's current mode and the source grant or arrangement's snapshotted mode. A historical monthly grant remains ineligible after an offer edit. Reject sources whose payment origin cannot be proven. Guard access-mode edits in the same lock order as eligibility changes so configuration cannot become invalid mid-confirmation.

### Calculation and minimum initial payment

Use integer centimes or PostgreSQL NUMERIC. Split the price into near-equal tranches, allocating remainder cents to the earliest installments. Apply credit, then any new money, to the earliest outstanding tranche. With a 440.00 DT target in three tranches, the scheduled amounts are 146.67, 146.67 and 146.66 DT.

| Eligible credit | New payment at activation | Remaining tranches after activation |
|---|---:|---|
| 100.00 DT | 46.67 DT | 146.67 + 146.66 DT |
| 180.00 DT | 0.00 DT | 113.34 + 146.66 DT |
| 300.00 DT | 0.00 DT | 140.00 DT |

These examples assume no higher configured minimum. Proposed rule for new schedules: the initial threshold is the greater of the first scheduled tranche and the existing configured minimum. Eligible credit satisfies that threshold first. If the threshold exceeds the first tranche, apply any new payment left after it to later tranches. A one-payment purchase requires the full uncovered price. Keep existing arrangements and legacy admin flows on their current rules. Credit equal to the target price can activate after explicit confirmation. Credit above the target price requires manual review, with no automatic transfer, wallet credit, or refund.
